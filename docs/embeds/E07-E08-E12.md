## E07: article layout, theme, responsive images and print

`../f1StoriesPage/blog-module/build/telemetry-figure.js` now emits two responsive `<picture>` elements: a light pair and a dark pair, each with narrow and wide sources. The article root uses `data-theme="light"` for light mode and no light attribute for dark mode. Scoped rules in `../f1StoriesPage/blog-module/blog/article-editorial.css` switch the pairs without JS, override only this figure's generic image-caption prefix, and keep all context/caption/source text in article HTML. Image dimensions reserve their aspect ratios; figures have no internal scroll area. Print forces the light image and includes the source, caption and analysis URL.

The browser check uses the generated/minified article styles from the Release A public build and the production figure renderer. It places two figures in the article's existing body-grid/content classes, then checks 320, 390, 768 and 1440px in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit. Every case has zero horizontal overflow; the displayed image fits the article column. Changing/removing the theme attribute switches the figure without changing the URL or localStorage. Print selects light and retains context, source, caption and analysis link. A JavaScript-disabled pass renders both static figures. The fixture page made zero external requests, including to OpenF1.

Chromium captures and measurements are in [article-release-a](article-release-a/). At 320px the article column is 304px and the image scales from a 360px narrow SVG; at 390px it is 374px. At 768px it is 734px and uses the 680px wide SVG; at 1440px the article measure is 680px. Thus the text scales up slightly at 768px and stays readable. With two figures at a mobile width, four image variants loaded (light and dark narrow for each figure); both theme variants can be fetched even though one is hidden. The narrow assets are about 15.1 KB each and the wide assets about 16.5 KB each, far below the E12 limit.
